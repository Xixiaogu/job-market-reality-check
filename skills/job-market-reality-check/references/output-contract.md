# Output contract

The final response must be decision-oriented.

## Required sections

1. Executive judgment: sample size, strongest conclusion, one limitation.
2. Market snapshot: role families, recurring skills, data-quality notes.
3. Application queue: apply now, stretch, prepare first, defer.
4. Evidence to emphasize: map projects to requirements without fabricating metrics.
5. Skill-gap priorities: at most three, ranked by value and tractability.
6. Two-week action plan: submissions, material improvements, one validation step.
7. Limits: coverage, missing fields, rule-based nature, no offer-probability claim.

## Tone

- direct;
- evidence-based;
- no motivational filler;
- no fake certainty;
- use the user's language.
